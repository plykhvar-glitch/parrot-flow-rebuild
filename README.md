# Growth Audit — Parrot

**Live:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/parrot-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Parrot already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Parrot's own design. Only the order of the screens changes. The art direction stays as it is.

Prepared by Adapty. Based on the live iOS flow and the App Store listing.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Name their goal on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Catch a closed paywall with the $44.99 annual plan | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 03 | Ask for reminders after the trial starts | Reminders | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Let the loader read their answers back | Loader | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put the 4.7 next to the price | Paywall | CR +10–15% |

Combined potential: CR to purchase up to +25%, revenue up to +30%.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote and claim comes from Parrot's own published material: the App Store listing (4.7 from 15K ratings, the in-app purchase list with Annual Parrot+ at $89.99 and $44.99 and Monthly Parrot+ at $19.99, the Marcus, UK review), parrotapp.com, and Parrot's in-app screens.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Parrot.
